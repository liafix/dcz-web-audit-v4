# Changelog

## 4.0.0 — High-End Revenue Diagnostic

### Added

- Executive Summary and Executive Preview.
- Money Leak Map with evidence, mechanism, blocked business step and recommended fix.
- deterministic business vertical and business model inference.
- ROI scenario builder with conservative, realistic and growth models.
- three-question qualification flow.
- contextual DCZ solution tiers: Conversion Foundation, Growth Funnel, Revenue Platform.
- relevant case-study registry with explicit proof type.
- scanner-safe booking confirmation and generic booking webhook/manual admin workflow.
- tokenized Opportunity Brief.
- Company Fit and Buying Intent scoring, Priority A routing and one-time alerting.
- service/marketing follow-up jobs, unsubscribe and stop conditions.
- revenue admin metrics, lead details and booking status management.
- Hostinger Node.js deployment and cron documentation.

### Security and reliability

- same-origin checks on sensitive POST operations.
- best-effort analytics separated from critical business operations.
- rate limits for revenue events, ROI, qualification, brief and booking.
- private headers/noindex for report, brief, booking, unsubscribe, admin and API routes.
- hash-only public access tokens and scanner-resistant confirmation steps.

### Product truthfulness

- no precise monetary claim without user inputs.
- ROI assumptions and disclaimer are mandatory.
- demo/concept/delivered/verified proof types are distinguished.
- product remains a preliminary homepage diagnostic, not a complete website crawl.
